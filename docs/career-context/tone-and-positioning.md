# Tone and Positioning

Voice, tone, and format rules live in voice-and-format.md, which is the authoritative source and the file the resume tailor sends to the model. Positioning lives in resume-master.md. This file exists to feed the emphasis focus chips in the tailor tool, so keep the section below in its current format. Director / Head of Design and Staff / Principal IC opportunities have equal priority in resume-master.md; chip order does not rank career preferences.

For Self-Employed / Consulting, make Seeens, nextchapter, and Esatto the central evidence for hands-on, AI-assisted end-to-end product design and building, in that order. Use resume-master.md for capabilities and release stages and experience-framing.md for the story. Seeens is a released closed alpha; nextchapter combines working tools with disabled live discovery; Esatto packages reusable agent workflows and checks. Do not infer client results, adoption, autonomous engineering, or measured speed gains. The emphasis options below select a lens, not new career facts.
